Independent JPEG Group (IJG) libjpeg
====================================

JUCE 8.0.14 embeds a reduced copy of the IJG libjpeg sources in
`modules/juce_graphics/image_formats/jpglib/`, and Juicy16's AU and VST3
binaries contain that code. This file reproduces the IJG terms verbatim from
that copy's own README.

As required by condition (2) below, and as stated in `NOTICE.md`:
this software is based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.

JUCE distributes a subset of the library and made source changes to it, listed
upstream in `modules/juce_graphics/image_formats/jpglib/changes to libjpeg for
JUCE.txt`: renamed `jpeg_entropy_decoder` and `jpeg_d_coef_controller` struct
members in `jpegint.h`, a commented-out duplicate `JPEG_MARKER` enum in
`jdmarker.c`, a commented-out `jmemsys.h` include in `jmemnobs.c`, and a
commented-out `_WIN32` ifdef in `jconfig.h`. Juicy16 makes no further changes.
The complete corresponding source is the pinned JUCE 8.0.14 release named in
`docs/DEPENDENCIES.md`.
