Juicy16 privacy

Juicy16 doesn't collect anything. There's no telemetry, analytics, accounts,
crash reporting or update checks, and it never connects to the internet (JUCE's
web and network features are compiled out).

It only reads the bank files you choose, or the ones a project you open refers
to. On macOS it saves a bookmark to that file in your project so it can find it
again. That lives wherever your DAW stores the project.

When it repairs a broken DLS file, it writes a fixed copy to your system's temp
folder and deletes it when it's no longer needed. Your original file is never
changed.

Nothing you load or play leaves your computer. If you send me a bug report,
that's your choice and only includes what you attach.

Your DAW, operating system and wherever you downloaded Juicy16 from have their own
privacy policies. This only covers Juicy16 itself.
